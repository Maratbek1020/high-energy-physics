Modern Experiments

Major experiments investigating the properties of matter at high energies.

ALICE

The ALICE experiment at the Large Hadron Collider studies the strong interaction and the properties of the quark-gluon plasma.

STAR

The STAR experiment at RHIC investigates the properties of nuclear matter and heavy-ion collisions.

NICA

NICA investigates dense baryonic matter and the phase structure of strongly interacting matter.

RESEARCH PROGRAMS

Heavy-Ion Physics

Heavy-ion collisions provide an opportunity to study strongly interacting matter under extreme conditions of temperature and density.

Pb+Pb

Lead-lead collisions at the LHC.

Xe+Xe

Xenon-xenon collisions used to study the properties of hot nuclear matter.

QGP

Study of the quark-gluon plasma created in high-energy nuclear collisions.

pT

Analysis of transverse-momentum spectra of identified particles.
