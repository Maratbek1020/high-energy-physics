ABOUT THE PROJECT

High Energy Physics

This scientific and educational portal is dedicated to modern high energy and nuclear physics.

The website provides educational materials on the Standard Model, quantum chromodynamics, quark-gluon plasma, heavy-ion collisions and transverse-momentum spectra.

It is intended for students, researchers and everyone interested in understanding the fundamental structure of matter and the physics of high-energy collisions.

The project also presents experimental facilities, theoretical models and methods used in the analysis of high-energy nuclear-collision data.

Research Topics

  • Heavy-Ion Collisions
  • Quantum Chromodynamics
