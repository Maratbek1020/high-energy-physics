THEORY AND DATA ANALYSIS

Physical Models

Models used to describe particle production, collision geometry and transverse-momentum spectra in high-energy nuclear collisions.

Glauber Model Geometry and centrality of nuclear collisions. The Glauber model is widely used to estimate the number of participating nucleons and binary nucleon-nucleon collisions.
Blast-Wave Model Description of collective radial flow and particle transverse-momentum spectra at kinetic freeze-out.
Tsallis Model A non-extensive statistical approach used to describe transverse-momentum distributions with power-law behavior at high transverse momentum.
Tsallis Blast-Wave Model A combination of the Tsallis statistical distribution and transverse collective flow. The model allows the simultaneous study of temperature, transverse flow and the non-extensivity parameter.
EXAMPLE

Transverse Momentum

The transverse momentum of a particle relative to the beam axis is defined as

\(p_T = \sqrt{p_x^2+p_y^2}\)

Transverse-momentum spectra provide important information about particle production, kinetic freeze-out and collective motion of matter created in high-energy nuclear collisions.
