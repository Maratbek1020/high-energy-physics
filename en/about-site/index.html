ABOUT THE WEBSITE

About the Site

A scientific and educational resource dedicated to modern high-energy and nuclear physics.

⚛

Scientific Content

The website contains educational and research materials covering particle physics, nuclear physics, QCD, QGP and heavy-ion collisions.

📊

Data Analysis

The site presents methods for analysing transverse-momentum spectra and studying properties of matter produced in high-energy nuclear collisions.

🔬

Experiments

Information about major high-energy physics experiments, including ALICE, STAR, NICA and BM@N.

🧮

Physical Models

The website introduces Glauber, Blast-Wave, Tsallis and Tsallis Blast-Wave models used in high-energy nuclear physics.

PROJECT GOAL

Physics • Research • Education

The main goal of this website is to provide a clear and accessible introduction to high-energy physics while also presenting modern research topics and data-analysis methods.

The resource is intended for students, researchers and anyone interested in the fundamental structure of matter.
